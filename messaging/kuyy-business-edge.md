# Kuyy! Business: the edge, and how to say it

Every @kuyy.business post carries at least one of these five. Written Oct 2026 from Priscilla's brief; check against the brand guideline's rules (approved numbers, no competitor names outside a sourced ledger, no exclamation marks).

## The five edges

| # | Edge | Say it like this | Not like this |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **More than software.** Other platforms give you tools to run a studio. Kuyy! gives you those tools plus a front door to new customers. | "Booking software, with demand built in." · "Other software gives you tools. Kuyy! Business also gives you a front door to new customers." | Naming Rezerv or Vibefam on the feed. |
| 2 | **Demand.** Kuyy! has a consumer app full of people already looking for classes and activities. Booking-only software cannot bring new customers. | "300,000+ registered users already looking for activities." · "Your studio gets seen by people who are searching." | "Your classes will be full." Any fill rate or booking number we don't have data for. "300K MAU" (not cleared, see below). |
| 3 | **Lower payment fees.** Competitors mostly use international payment gateways; Kuyy! connects to banks directly. Lead with BCA VA. | "Lower bank transfer fees, like BCA VA: we connect to banks directly." Always with a dated source line on the slide. | "Lowest fees", "cheapest". Any card or instalment fee claim. |
| 4 | **Your own website, no app, no account.** Every studio gets a page (yourstudio.kuyy.id) where members book and pay without downloading an app or creating an account. | "Your own website. No app needed." · "Members book with no app and no account." | Implying it replaces a custom domain, if it doesn't. |
| 5 | **Simple to use.** The interface is made to be learned without training. | Show it: a real screen, a short task. "Built to be simple." | "The simplest", "the easiest" (a superlative we can't prove). |

The one-line version for internal use and investors: **Kuyy! = ClassPass + Rezerv** (a booking system and the customers to fill it, in one). On the feed, describe the outcome instead of naming the brands: Indonesian studio owners mostly don't know ClassPass, and the guideline bars competitor names outside a sourced ledger.

## Things that stay off the feed

- **"Kuyy! owns the customer data."** It's the internal reason booking-only tools can't send studios new customers, but a studio owner reading it hears "Kuyy! takes my members". Say "people already looking for classes on the Kuyy! app" instead.
- **MAU.** The brief says 300K+ MAU; the approved number is 300,000+ *registered users*, and MAU (200K+ in the knowledge base) is not cleared for the feed. Use registered users until a current MAU figure is signed off.
- **Competitor names** (Rezerv, Vibefam, ClassPass), except in a LedgerCompare with a public, dated source.

## Open facts to confirm

- Who pays the Rp 2,500 bank VA fee (studio or member), and the current Xendit public VA rate with its date.
- Whether yourstudio.kuyy.id is on every plan. The price list puts "website builder" on Business only.
- That guest booking (no account) works end to end, and that the booking-only tools studios compare us with really do require an account.
