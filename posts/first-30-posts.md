## Read this before you post anything

**1. Anything marked 🔒 HOLD can't go out yet.** 9 of the 30 posts need real data, real photos or a fact confirmed first. The "Blockers" section at the end lists each one and who has to supply it.

**2. I could not open business.kuyy.id.** This environment's network policy blocks both `business.kuyy.id` and `kuyy.id`. The site facts below come from your three files plus a search-engine snippet of `kuyy.id/business`. Two things in that snippet don't match your files:

| Topic | Your guideline (approved, Sep 2026) | Indexed site snippet (date unknown) | What I did |
|---|---|---|---|
| Communities / cities | 4,000+ communities, 50+ cities | "2,000 sports communities", "30+ other cities" | Used the guideline numbers. The site copy looks out of date: update it before paid traffic lands there. |
| Rp 2,500 transaction fee | Knowledge base: the studio's bank VA fee | "Rp 2,500 fee per transaction **paid by the participant**", "zero commission, 100% of ticket price" | The fee ledgers in posts 04 and 22 assume the studio pays the fee. **If the participant pays it, both ledgers have to be reframed before they go out.** |

**3. The locked system forces some repetition.** There are 3 cover layouts per ground, and the grid alternates grounds, so a strict rotation gives exactly 5 of each: 5 PriceSheets, 5 BigNumbers, 5 ProductScreens, 5 FounderNotes, 5 Studio cases, 5 Math carousels. Five price posts in 30 days is a lot for a new account. The rotation rule is what forces it; I've given each one a different angle. If you'd rather post fewer, relaxing the rotation rule is a design-system decision for you, not something I should do silently.

**4. Only 5 BigNumber topics are allowed.** BigNumber may only use approved numbers, and there are exactly 5 that fit in 8 characters: 300,000+, 4,000+, 400,000+, 50+ and 30 days. All 5 are used here, so the next BigNumber cycle needs a newly approved number.
